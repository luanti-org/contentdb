title: Password Reset Required
description: What to do when ContentDB asks you to reset your password

As a security precaution, you need to choose a new password before you can sign
in to your ContentDB account. Your old password will no longer work.

## If your account has an email address

Use [Forgot my password](/user/forgot-password/) to get a link to reset your password.

If you attempted to log in with your email address, you might have received a
password reset already.

## If your account is linked to GitHub

[Sign in with GitHub](/github/start/), then choose a new password.

## If none of these work

If your account doesn't have an email address and isn't linked to GitHub, or you
no longer have access to them, [contact us](/help/contact_us/). We'll need to
confirm that the account is yours before we can help.
